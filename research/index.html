Research

I am an ML engineer and Ph.D. researcher at Cornell working on computer vision, multimodal sensing, and geospatial AI for agriculture and R&D. My work spans 3D point-cloud and multispectral phenotyping, root segmentation, UAV weed detection, and predictive modeling, with a focus on translating complex biological data into decision-ready workflows.

Research focus areas

  • Computer Vision for Agriculture

    YOLO-based UAV pipelines for weed detection and plot-level counting.

  • Multispectral & 3D Phenotyping

    PlantEye point clouds and multispectral data for plant stress and herbicide response.

  • Root Phenotyping

    SegFormer and U-Net models for root segmentation and trait extraction.

  • Geospatial & Climate AI

    Maize GxE trial optimization and climate-analogue mapping with remote sensing.

  • Decision-Ready ML for R&D

    Reproducible pipelines that turn sensor data into objective R&D endpoints.
