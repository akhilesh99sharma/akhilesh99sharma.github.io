Selected Work

A selection of research and engineering projects in computer vision, scientific imaging and applied AI for agriculture and the environment.

  • Weed Detection

    Computer Vision

    UAV-based weed detection at Kansas State University and Cornell (2022–2024). YOLO field pipelines for multi-species detection and plot-level counting, with drone counts showing >0.99 correlation with manual counts for key weed species.

  • Early Plant Stress Detection

    Multimodal ML

    Non-destructive multispectral models that distinguish contrasting Palmer amaranth populations before herbicide application and track treatment response afterward, with Bayer Crop Science and Cornell University.

  • Root Architecture Quantification

    Scientific Imaging

    SegFormer and U-Net models for multiclass root segmentation and automated trait extraction at Cornell (2026), reaching clDice = 0.987 on held-out images. The pipeline turns root images into quantitative root system architecture traits for plant biology research.

  • VulneraSafe AI

    AI Systems

    A multi-pathogen, vulnerability-aware, One Health food-safety early-warning and decision-support platform for the 2026 NAFSI Hub SENS-D Data Challenge. It models Salmonella, Listeria and STEC risk and pairs it with safety-constrained operational optimization for a regional grocery retail chain.

  • Climate Analogue Optimization

    Geospatial AI

    LLM-assisted climate-ready maize breeding and GxE trial optimization (2026–Present, Cornell Atkinson Center for Sustainability fellowship). A computational framework testing whether today’s U.S. maize trial environments represent the heat, drought and compound stress expected in the 2030s and 2050s.
